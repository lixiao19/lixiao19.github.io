ABOUT ME

About me.

High-performance networks for AI systems.

I am a Ph.D. student in the Department of Computer Science and Technology at Tsinghua University, where I began my doctoral studies in 2023. I received my B.S. from the same department in 2023.

My research interests span programmable networks, high-performance networking, and distributed systems for AI. My recent work focuses on cross-datacenter RDMA, network performance diagnosis, load balancing, and collective communications.
