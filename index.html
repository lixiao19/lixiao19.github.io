About me.关于我

Datacenter networking systems.数据中心网络系统。

I am a Ph.D. student in the Department of Computer Science and Technology at Tsinghua University, advised by Prof. Mingwei Xu. I began my doctoral studies in 2023, after receiving my B.S. from the same department that year.我是清华大学计算机科学与技术系的博士生，导师为徐明伟教授。我于2023年开始攻读博士学位，此前于同年在该系获得学士学位。

My research focuses on datacenter networking systems, spanning areas such as programmable networks, RDMA networks, and distributed systems for AI. I am currently working to improve the performance of long-distance RDMA transport to support growing communication demands across datacenters.我的研究领域是数据中心网络系统，涵盖可编程网络、RDMA网络和面向AI的分布式系统等。近期，我正在探索跨数据中心的长距离RDMA传输性能优化，以支持不断增长的跨数据中心通信需求。
